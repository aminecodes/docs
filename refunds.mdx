**Last updated: 18 November 2026.**

This policy explains refunds and subscription cancellation for Essal LLC's business software. It covers software bought through Paddle and directly from Essal, with the purchase-channel differences below. The version provided at purchase applies to that transaction, subject to mandatory rights.

## 14-day initial-purchase guarantee

You may request a full refund of your first paid purchase of an Essal software subscription within **14 calendar days of that payment**. For a trial that converts to a paid subscription, the period starts with the first payment, not the start of the free trial. A request received within the period remains timely while it is being processed.

The guarantee does not require that you have avoided using the software. It applies once to the initial purchase of the relevant subscription, not to renewal charges, upgrades or add-ons unless the offer expressly includes them. We may investigate fraud or deliberate guarantee abuse; this never removes mandatory rights. A separately negotiated enterprise variation must be agreed and disclosed before purchase.

## If you bought through Paddle

Paddle is the seller and Merchant of Record for a purchase identified as a Paddle transaction. You can request a refund through the support link in your receipt or at [paddle.net](https://paddle.net). You can also contact [billing@essal.it.com](mailto:billing@essal.it.com) for help; we will communicate an agreed refund to Paddle for processing. **We do not pay Paddle refunds directly to you.**

Our initial-purchase guarantee is additional to [Paddle's buyer terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms), [Paddle's refund policy](https://www.paddle.com/legal/refund-policy) and non-waivable legal rights. Paddle retains its contractual discretion and obligations to cancel or refund transactions, including renewal transactions. An Essal restriction cannot prevent a refund Paddle is entitled or required to provide.

Paddle processes an approved refund to the original payment method where possible, subject to its current refund policy. Paddle's policy describes its processing time; your bank or payment provider may affect when you see the credit. We do not promise a separate payment-provider settlement time.

## If you bought directly from Essal

Email [billing@essal.it.com](mailto:billing@essal.it.com) with your company name, contact address, invoice or order reference, purchase date and the subscription concerned. A reason is useful to improve the software but is not a condition of the initial-purchase guarantee. Never email full card details, passwords or unnecessary personal data.

For an eligible request, Essal will arrange the refund through the original payment route where available and confirm the amount and processing status. Direct enterprise orders may expressly vary the voluntary guarantee. Fees for separately scoped professional services are governed by their signed statement of work; this software guarantee does not invent a refund right for work already commissioned under a different agreement.

## Cancel future renewal

For Paddle subscriptions, use the manage-subscription link in your confirmation email or [Paddle buyer support](https://paddle.net). For a direct subscription, email billing using the account's authorised business contact and identify the subscription to cancel.

Cancellation normally prevents the next renewal and takes effect at the end of the current paid period. It does not, by itself, refund charges already paid. Any cancellation and notice provisions in a signed direct enterprise order must be disclosed before purchase. Where we receive a timely request through the stated cancellation channel, internal processing delay must not cause a further renewal charge.

## Renewals, errors and defects

The initial-purchase guarantee does not automatically repeat at renewal. Nevertheless, duplicate charges, billing errors, unauthorised transactions, material non-delivery and product defects must be investigated. Contact support for product defects and billing for purchase issues. This policy does not waive statutory remedies or Paddle's refund authority and does not impose a universal "no refunds" rule.

Prorated refunds for unused time are not automatic unless the order, an agreed remedy, Paddle's rules or applicable law provides them. If you believe a charge is wrong, contact us or Paddle promptly. Nothing here removes a lawful right to dispute a payment with your payment provider.

## Access and records after refund

A full refund normally ends the refunded software entitlement. Where feasible, discuss an available export with support before access ends. Customer data is handled under the [data processing agreement](/data-processing); refunding a payment does not authorise retention for an unrelated purpose or require unlawful destruction of accounting records.

If your request is unresolved, follow the [complaints process](/complaints). The rights applicable to the transaction and the relevant policy version are preserved.
