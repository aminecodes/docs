This proposed policy applies to your organisation and authorised users of Essal and Essal Work software. It forms part of the software agreement when validly incorporated. It is a review draft and is not a claim that Paddle has approved any particular product.

## Authorised business use

Use the software within the agreed business purpose, features and limits. Your organisation must have authority for the data it submits, the users it invites, systems it connects and instructions it gives. Obtain required permissions and provide required notices, especially for employees, candidates, visitors, location information and training participants.

Do not use the service to:

- Break applicable law, sanctions, contractual rights or intellectual-property rights.
- Access systems or personal data without authority, steal credentials, spread malware or exploit vulnerabilities without written permission.
- Conduct unlawful monitoring, discriminatory decision-making, harassment, threats or abuse.
- Send spam or unsolicited bulk messages, impersonate others unlawfully, or bypass a recipient's lawful choices.
- Evade access limits, share credentials outside the agreed account, compromise service integrity or disrupt other customers.
- Sell prohibited goods or services, operate an unauthorised marketplace or payment service, or use software fees as disguised payments for another activity.

## Product responsibilities

**Access:** use employee, visitor and facility records only with appropriate authority. Buying software does not purchase physical hardware or authorise surveillance.

**Reach:** apply appropriate notices and controls to customer visits, contact information and location data. Do not track people beyond the authorised business purpose.

**Talent:** your organisation controls recruitment purposes and decisions. Do not use applicant data for an unrelated purpose, scrape it unlawfully or use the platform to provide manpower or recruitment-agency services outside your authority.

**Asset and Project:** restrict access to custody, project, customer and collaboration records according to business need. Do not upload unnecessary sensitive information.

**Guard:** run security-awareness simulations only for organisations, accounts and domains you are authorised to test. Limit simulations to an approved scope and audience; do not use them for real credential theft, unauthorised phishing or third-party compromise. Training and completion records are not an independent certification of legal compliance.

Your organisation must review consequential decisions itself and comply with applicable data-protection requirements. A software subscription does not expand Essal's licence or your organisation's authority.

## Paddle-related restrictions

Transactions through Paddle must satisfy [Paddle's acceptable-use policy](https://www.paddle.com/help/start/intro-to-paddle/what-am-i-not-allowed-to-sell-on-paddle), its supplier and buyer agreements, and applicable laws. Software-related services are not automatically eligible just because they appear on an invoice with software. An offering must be accurately described and eligible in its own right.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report suspected abuse, unlawful content or an infringement to [legal@essal.it.com](mailto:legal@essal.it.com). Include the affected service, specific location or reference, a description and evidence you are authorised to share. Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive data. We may ask for clarification and a response from the affected customer where appropriate.

We may investigate, remove or restrict affected content or access, preserve evidence where lawful, and cooperate with competent authorities. Measures must be proportionate; where practicable and lawful, we will explain the issue and allow correction. Use the [complaints process](/complaints) to challenge a decision. This process does not remove statutory remedies.
