## Existing agreements

Until a valid transition is completed, the agreement and notice applicable to your relationship remain relevant. Existing published material is available at the [previous legal center](https://legal.essal.cloud/). This reference preserves access to historical material; it does not endorse that material as an adequate replacement for the proposed updates.

## Future publication and changes

At release, each policy must show its actual effective date and version. Preserve the exact released text in a dated archive and record material changes here. Material contractual changes require the appropriate notice and acceptance or transition process; publication alone must not silently rewrite a signed enterprise contract or retrospectively create consent.

For the agreement or notice relevant to an earlier purchase, contact [legal@essal.it.com](mailto:legal@essal.it.com) with the purchase date and reference. Do not include full payment-card details.
