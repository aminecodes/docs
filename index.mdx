## Company information

**Essal LLC** is a limited liability company registered in the Qatar Financial Centre, **QFC number 02195**. Our registered office address supplied for these drafts is **9th floor, QFC Tower 1, Westbay, Doha, Qatar**.

These policies cover `essal.qa`, `essal.it.com`, `essal.cloud`, `admin.essal.cloud` and `essal.work` where Essal LLC provides the relevant service. A separately identified third-party service remains subject to its own terms and privacy information.

## Buying through Paddle

When your checkout identifies Paddle as the seller, Paddle is the reseller and Merchant of Record for that transaction. Essal supplies and supports the software. [Paddle's buyer terms](https://www.paddle.com/legal/buyer-terms) govern your purchase from Paddle; [our terms](/terms) govern software use. Direct enterprise purchases are identified in the signed order.
