## What to include

Describe the relevant product or website, your business relationship, the order/ticket reference if available, what happened and the outcome you seek. Include a contact address for a response. Do not email passwords, full payment-card details, unrelated applicant/employee records or identity documents unless specifically required through an appropriate secure process.

We may ask for proportionate information to confirm identity or authority. We will route the concern to the relevant team, investigate, and explain the outcome and any further steps. If you disagree, reply to legal with the original reference and why you seek review. We do not promise a support response time that has not been agreed in an SLA.

## Privacy requests and external complaints

The [privacy policy](/privacy) explains rights and applicable response periods. Under the QFC framework a response is ordinarily required within 30 days, with any permitted extension explained within the initial period. A customer's employee or applicant request may need to be handled by that customer as controller, with Essal's assistance.

You may complain directly to the **QFC Data Protection Office** using the current channels on its [official website](https://www.qfc.qa/en/operate/data-protection/). You do not have to complete Essal's internal complaint process before exercising a statutory complaint right.

## Paddle transactions

For a Paddle purchase, use the support link in your receipt or [paddle.net](https://paddle.net) for payment and transaction assistance. Essal handles product support and can help communicate an agreed refund to Paddle. Review the [refund policy](/refunds) and Paddle's buyer terms for your applicable rights. Contacting support does not remove a lawful payment-dispute right.
